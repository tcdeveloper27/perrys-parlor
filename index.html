SPIDER SOLITAIRE · EST. 1885

Pull up a chair, partner.

Spider Solitaire dealt on green felt in an Old West saloon parlor, with ragtime on the player piano, painted frontier face cards, and a family of spiders who drop by when you win — but never every time.

  • One, two, three or four suits, plus Spiderette and Relaxed Spider.
  • "Winnable hands only" — every hand dealt has been solved first, so it can be won.
  • Unlimited Undo, a Hint that shows you the move, and "Finish it for me".
  • Hand numbers: play the very same deal as a friend and compare scores — no internet needed.
  • Big, clear cards and buttons. Use your own photos as card backs.
  • Free. No ads. No account. Works offline.
