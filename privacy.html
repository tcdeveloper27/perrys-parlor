Perry's Parlor

PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026; updated October 4, 2026. This policy covers the Perry's Parlor game for Android and Windows, made by Legion Software.

The short version

Perry's Parlor does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

On Windows only, the game's About the Parlor page has two buttons, "Visit faleofaz.org" and "Donate with Venmo", for a Utah care home. Clicking one opens that page in your own web browser; the game itself sends nothing, and nothing happens unless you click.

What stays on your device

To play, the game keeps a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • the names typed into the Guest Book (for example "Grandpa");
  • your scores, statistics, settings and the game in progress;
  • if you choose to use photos as card backs, a cropped copy of each picture you choose.

None of this ever leaves your device. On Android it is removed when you uninstall the game. On Windows it stays in your own user folder (%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Perry's Parlor) so that reinstalling keeps your games and scores; delete that folder to remove it. You can remove a photo at any time from Settings → My Photos → Remove.

Photos

The game never looks through your photos. When you tap "Choose a Photo", your device's own picker opens (on a computer, the usual Open window) and the game receives only the picture you pick. The game asks for no permissions.

Clipboard

"Send a Telegram" copies a short score message to your clipboard, only when you tap it, so you can paste it into a message yourself. The game sends nothing.

Children

The game is made for adults but collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer contact email shown on the Perry's Parlor page in the Google Play Store.
